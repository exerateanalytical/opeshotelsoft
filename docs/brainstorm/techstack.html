Pros

  • Filament gives you admin panels in days not months
  • Livewire feels reactive without a heavy JS framework

Cons

  • Livewire can feel slow for complex real-time UIs
  • Guest-facing portal needs a richer frontend experience
  • Less suitable for a white-label SaaS product later
My recommendation: Option A (Laravel + Next.js + Flutter). You get Laravel's battle-tested business logic for the heavy HMS domain (reservations, billing, loyalty ledgers), Next.js for a world-class guest-facing web experience, and Flutter for a truly native mobile app — all supported by your existing Laragon environment. This is the closest to what Hilton and Marriott use at enterprise scale, just without the 10-year legacy debt.
